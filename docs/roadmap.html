Roadmap

What is being built, and what is only an idea.

Ember is one person’s project, so this is an order of intent rather than a schedule. Nothing below carries a date, and things move backwards as often as forwards. If something you need is missing, open an issue — that is the whole voting system, and it works.

In progress 0
Nothing is mid-flight. v1.3.2 shipped on Oct 4; whatever is picked up next will appear here.
Next up 4

Sign the installers

Windows shows a “protected your PC” box and macOS needs a Terminal command before it will open, because the builds are not code signed. Certificates fix both, and it is the single biggest thing standing between Ember and someone trying it.

Rough edgeDesktop

Turn Google sign-in on in the public builds

Calendar sync and Drive backup are written and working, but the downloads ship without the Google application key they need, so both are off. Supplying one to the release build switches them on for everybody.

Rough edgeSync

Recurrence exceptions in the Calendar sync

Moving one occurrence of a routine works inside Ember, but the sync writes the whole series’ time, so Google never hears about it. Recurrence exceptions are the proper fix.

Known gapSync

Tests for the date and recurrence logic

The checks cover conventions and colour contrast thoroughly and the actual logic hardly at all — which is where the bugs have been. Dates, recurrence and filtering need real coverage.

Under the hood
Backlog 6

Your planner on more than one machine

There is no server, so there is no sync. Backing up to a file and restoring works, but it is not the same thing. The honest options are syncing through a folder you already own, or Drive doing the job properly once sign-in is on.

Most askedSync

A layout that actually works on a phone

The page folds down to a narrow screen, but folding is not designing, and without sync there would be nothing on the phone to look at. This waits behind the one above it.

Most askedMobile

Tasks that repeat

Routines repeat; tasks have one date. A quarterly invoice is neither a habit nor a one-off, and right now it has to be typed again every time.

Known gapTasks

Choose which Google calendar it writes to

The sync writes into your primary calendar. Anyone keeping work in a second calendar wants to pick, and anyone sharing one definitely does.

Sync

Bring a planner in from somewhere else

Starting from an empty planner is a real cost when your tasks are already in Todoist, Notion or a spreadsheet. An importer for a CSV would cover most of it.

Idea

Print a week, or export one

No print stylesheet and no way out to CSV. Some people want the week on paper on a Monday morning, and some want their own data in a spreadsheet.

Shipped 11

Attachments open in the app

PDFs, images, plain text and spreadsheets show inside Ember instead of being handed to whatever your computer would open them with.

v1.3.2

The board across all workspaces

One board per workspace, stacked and collapsible, rather than the board being switched off there. Lanes are never merged by name.

v1.3.2

Routines that repeat monthly

On a day of the month, which is what a bill is. A month with no 31st uses its last day rather than skipping.

v1.3.1

The dashboard follows your accent

All five sections take the colour you picked, told apart by weight rather than by hue.

v1.3.1

Workspaces

Up to six separate planners in one window, each with its own categories, lanes, tasks, routines and notes — plus a view across all of them.

v1.3.0

The dashboard reworked

Three columns that each scroll on their own, so a packed schedule can no longer push what is overdue off the bottom of the page.

v1.2.3

Drag a calendar block to another day

Pick up a task, a routine or a blocked-out hour and drop it where it belongs. A routine asks which occurrences you meant.

v1.2.3

Quiet hours, a night at a time

Each night of the week keeps its own window, so weekends can start later than Tuesdays.

v1.2.3

Streaks with no ceiling

The whole history counts, not the last year — with a log that draws your year as twelve calendars, and days you can let off.

v1.2.2

Ember

A new name, a flame for a mark, and a warm accent the whole planner is built around. Setup became a page of its own, lit by one fire.

v1.2.0

Backups to a folder you chose

Not Downloads. Pick a folder once, by hand or weekly, and Ember says whether the write actually landed.

v1.1.0
How something gets onto this board. Mostly by someone hitting it and saying so. There is no upvote button here because a static page cannot count votes honestly — so the issue tracker is the board that counts. A thumbs-up on an issue is a vote, and a sentence about what you were trying to do when it got in your way is worth ten of them.
